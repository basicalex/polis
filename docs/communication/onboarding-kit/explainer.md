# 60-second explainer — video script

For a short video on the municipality's website and social channels. Around 60 seconds at an unhurried pace. Ten shots.

Shoot it plainly: a phone, a street, an office, a printed record. No stock footage of skylines, no animated data.

---

## English

**Title card:** How to report a problem to [Municipality]

| # | On screen | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A resident stands on a street, looking at a [category] problem. She takes out her phone. | You see a problem in your street. You report it. Then what? |
| 2 | Her phone shows the municipality's website. She taps the report form. | You file it on [web address], on the same site you already use. |
| 3 | The form: category, description, location. She types. | You choose the category, write what the problem is, and say where it is. |
| 4 | The screen shows a report number. She screenshots it. | Your report gets a number, and it gets a public record. |
| 5 | The record on screen. A line names the responsible office. | [Municipality] names the office responsible. That name is on the record. |
| 6 | The same record, one line lower: what will be done, by when. | The office writes down what it will do and by when. |
| 7 | A different person, somewhere else entirely — not part of that office — reads the evidence and the promise side by side. | Nothing is marked done until someone outside that office checks the evidence. |
| 8 | The record updates. A public page, open on a screen anyone can see. | Then the record is published. Anyone can read it. |
| 9 | The resident's own form, with her name and phone number greyed out. | Your name, your contact details, and what you wrote stay private. |
| 10 | Her phone again. She types her report number and sees the current status. **End card:** [Municipality] · [web address] | You can check your report at any time at [web address]. |

## Hrvatski

**Naslovna kartica:** Kako prijaviti problem — [Općina]

| # | Na ekranu | Naracija |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Stanovnica stoji na ulici i gleda problem iz kategorije [kategorija]. Vadi mobitel. | Vidite problem u svojoj ulici. Prijavite ga. Što se događa dalje? |
| 2 | Na mobitelu je web stranica općine. Otvara obrazac za prijavu. | Prijavu šaljete na [web adresa], na istoj stranici koju već koristite. |
| 3 | Obrazac: kategorija, opis, lokacija. Stanovnica upisuje. | Odaberete kategoriju, opišete problem i napišete gdje se nalazi. |
| 4 | Na ekranu se pojavljuje broj prijave. Stanovnica ga slika. | Vaša prijava dobiva broj i javni zapis. |
| 5 | Zapis na ekranu. Jedan redak imenuje nadležni ured. | [Općina] određuje nadležni ured. Njegov naziv piše u zapisu. |
| 6 | Isti zapis, redak niže: što će se učiniti i do kada. | Nadležni ured upisuje obvezu: što će učiniti i do kada. |
| 7 | Druga osoba, na drugom mjestu — izvan nadležnog ureda — uspoređuje dokaze i obvezu. | Prijava nije riješena dok dokaze ne provjeri netko izvan nadležnog ureda. |
| 8 | Zapis se mijenja. Javna stranica, otvorena na ekranu koji svatko može vidjeti. | Zatim se zapis objavljuje. Svatko ga može pročitati. |
| 9 | Njezin obrazac, s imenom i brojem telefona zasjenjenima. | Vaše ime, Vaš kontakt i ono što ste napisali ostaju privatni. |
| 10 | Ponovno mobitel. Upisuje broj prijave i vidi trenutačno stanje. **Završna kartica:** [Općina] · [web adresa] | Stanje svoje prijave možete provjeriti u svakom trenutku na [web adresa]. |
