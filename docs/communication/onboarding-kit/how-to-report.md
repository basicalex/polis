# Flyer — one page

For the notice board, the counter, and print. One side of A4. Everything below the rule is the flyer copy.

---

## English

# How to report a problem

You saw a problem in [Municipality]. Report it at [web address].

### How to file

1. Open [web address].
2. Choose the category — for example [category].
3. Write what the problem is and where it is.
4. Leave a contact so we can reach you.
5. Send it. You get a report number.

Keep the report number. You can use it to check the status at any time.

### What happens next

1. **Your report.** It is received and gets a record.
2. **Responsibility.** The office responsible is named. The name is on the record.
3. **Response.** That office writes down what it will do and by when.
4. **Independent check.** Someone outside that office checks the evidence.
5. **Public receipt.** The record is published.

A report is not done until the independent check confirms it.

### Where to see the record

The public records are at [web address]. Each record shows which office is responsible, what was promised and by when, and what changed the status.

Your name, your contact details, and what you wrote are not published.

## Hrvatski

# Kako prijaviti problem

Uočili ste problem u [Općina]. Prijavite ga na [web adresa].

### Kako se prijavljuje

1. Otvorite [web adresa].
2. Odaberite kategoriju — na primjer [kategorija].
3. Napišite što je problem i gdje se nalazi.
4. Ostavite kontakt kako bismo Vam se mogli javiti.
5. Pošaljite prijavu. Dobit ćete broj prijave.

Sačuvajte broj prijave. S njim u svakom trenutku možete provjeriti stanje.

### Što se događa poslije

1. **Vaša prijava.** Prijava je zaprimljena i dobiva zapis.
2. **Odgovornost.** Određuje se nadležni ured. Njegov naziv piše u zapisu.
3. **Odgovor.** Taj ured upisuje obvezu: što će učiniti i do kada.
4. **Neovisna provjera.** Dokaze provjerava netko izvan nadležnog ureda.
5. **Javna potvrda.** Zapis se objavljuje.

Prijava nije riješena dok to neovisna provjera ne potvrdi.

### Gdje možete vidjeti zapis

Javna evidencija nalazi se na [web adresa]. U svakom zapisu piše koji je ured nadležan, što je obećano i do kada te što je promijenilo stanje.

Vaše ime, Vaš kontakt i ono što ste napisali ne objavljuju se.

## Note for whoever prepares the print file

- Generate the QR code from the final `[web address]` and drop it in the marked box. Print it at least 2 cm wide so a phone reads it from arm's length.
- If residents can also report at the counter or by phone, add that line under "How to file". It is not in the copy because it differs by municipality.
- Body type no smaller than 11 pt. The flyer has to be readable on a notice board and by someone standing in the rain.
