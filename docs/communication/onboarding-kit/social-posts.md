# Three posts for the municipality's page

Written for Facebook. Post them in this order, a few days apart. Each one is short enough to read without tapping "See more".

---

## Post 1 — This is how you reach us now

### English

You can now report a problem to [Municipality] at [web address].

You write what the problem is and where it is. The report goes to the office responsible for it. That office writes down what it will do and by when.

Every report gets a record you can look up. The record shows which office is responsible and what was promised.

Your name and your contact details are not published.

A [category] problem in your street: [web address]

Svaka prijava dobiva zapis koji možete provjeriti. U zapisu piše koji je ured nadležan i što je obećano.

Vaše ime i Vaš kontakt ne objavljuju se.

Problem iz kategorije [kategorija] u Vašoj ulici: [web adresa]

## Post 2 — How you know it is done

### English

When a report is marked done, someone outside the responsible office has checked the evidence first.

The office that made the promise cannot mark its own work finished.

The check compares the evidence with what was promised. If the evidence does not cover the promise, the status does not change.

What stays behind is a public record: which office was responsible, what it promised, and what changed the status.

Read the records: [web address]

### Hrvatski

Kada je prijava označena kao riješena, dokaze je prije toga provjerio netko izvan nadležnog ureda.

Ured koji je preuzeo obvezu ne može sam ocijeniti da je posao gotov.

Neovisna provjera uspoređuje dokaze s onim što je obećano. Ako dokazi ne pokrivaju obvezu, stanje se ne mijenja.

Ostaje javni zapis: koji je ured bio nadležan, što je obećao i što je promijenilo stanje.

Pogledajte javnu evidenciju: [web adresa]

## Post 3 — How to file, step by step

### English

How to report a problem to [Municipality]:

1. Open [web address].
2. Choose the category — for example [category].
3. Write what the problem is and where it is.
4. Leave a contact so we can reach you. It is not published.
5. Send it. You get a report number.

Keep the number. You can use it to check the status at any time.

What happens after you send it:

1. Your report gets a record.
2. We name the office responsible.
3. That office states what it will do and by when.
4. An independent check confirms the evidence.
5. The record is published.

[web address]

### Hrvatski

Kako prijaviti problem [Općina]:

1. Otvorite [web adresa].
2. Odaberite kategoriju — na primjer [kategorija].
3. Napišite što je problem i gdje se nalazi.
4. Ostavite kontakt kako bismo Vam se mogli javiti. Ne objavljuje se.
5. Pošaljite prijavu. Dobit ćete broj prijave.

Sačuvajte broj prijave. S njim u svakom trenutku možete provjeriti stanje.

Što se događa nakon što pošaljete prijavu:

1. Vaša prijava dobiva zapis.
2. Određujemo nadležni ured.
3. Taj ured upisuje što će učiniti i do kada.
4. Neovisna provjera potvrđuje dokaze.
5. Zapis se objavljuje.

[web adresa]
